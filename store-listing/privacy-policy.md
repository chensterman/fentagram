# Fentagram privacy policy

Fentagram is a Chrome extension. It runs while you are signed into your own Instagram account.

## What it stores

A sync reads the usernames on your followers list and your following list, and the profile pictures shown next to those names. It saves the usernames, a small copy of each picture, and the time of the sync in Chrome’s local storage on your computer.

## What happens to it

That data stays in this browser. It is not sent to the developer or to any other server. It is not sold. It is not used for ads. The extension does not ask for your Instagram password.

## How to delete it

Uninstall Fentagram, or remove its data from Chrome. That deletes the saved lists and pictures from this browser.
